# Getting around Seoul

## Subway
Seoul's subway is the easiest way to get around for most visitors. Stations and
in-car announcements are in Korean and English, and lines are color-coded and
numbered. Signage includes station numbers, so you can navigate by number even if
you can't read the name. Trains run frequently throughout the day; service is
reduced late at night and stops after midnight, so check the last-train time for
your line when you are out late. For exact fares and operating hours, check the
official Seoul Metro information, as these can change.

## T-money card
A T-money card is a rechargeable transit card accepted on the subway, city buses,
and many taxis. You can buy one and top it up with cash at convenience stores and
at machines in subway stations. Tap the card on the reader when you enter and again
when you exit the subway. Using a transit card is cheaper and more convenient than
buying single-journey tickets each time, and it also gives transfer discounts when
you switch between subway and bus within a set time.

## Buses
City buses are color-coded by route type (for example, blue for main routes, green
for shorter neighborhood routes). Pay with a T-money card by tapping on boarding and
again when getting off to receive transfer discounts. Bus stop signs and apps show
arrival times. Buses can be faster than the subway for some trips but are harder to
navigate without a map app.

## Taxis
Taxis are widely available and metered. You can hail one on the street, find them at
taxi stands, or call one through a ride-hailing app. Many taxis accept T-money cards
and credit cards in addition to cash. There is a late-night surcharge during certain
hours. Having your destination written in Korean or shown on a map helps, as not all
drivers speak English.

## Airport to the city
Incheon International Airport connects to central Seoul by the AREX airport railroad,
by limousine buses that stop at major hotels and districts, and by taxi. Gimpo Airport
is closer to the city and is connected by subway and AREX. Choose based on your luggage,
budget, and destination. For current schedules and fares, check the official airport
and AREX information.

## Navigation apps
Google Maps has limited walking and driving directions in Korea, but local apps such as
Naver Map and KakaoMap give reliable public-transit and walking directions in English.
For door-to-door routing within Seoul, a local map app is usually the most accurate.
